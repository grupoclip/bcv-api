How to give credit

One visible line near where you show the rates is enough:

Source: Banco Central de Venezuela, via BCV Today (https://bcv.today)

On a web page, with links:

Source: Banco Central de Venezuela, via <a href="https://bcv.today">BCV Today</a>
(<a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/">CC BY 4.0</a>)

In Markdown, for example in a README:

Source: Banco Central de Venezuela, via [BCV Today](https://bcv.today)
([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/))

Depending on how you use the data:

  • In an app or site that shows the rates: a visible credit or a link to bcv.today next to the figures, or on the app's about or credits screen.
  • In a service with no interface (a backend, a bot or an integration that consumes the API): the credit in the project's documentation or README.
  • If you modify the data (for example, rounding or rates derived from these): say so, for example “derived from BCV Today data”.

The BCV rates

The exchange rates themselves are official public facts published by Banco Central de Venezuela. BCV Today claims no rights over those figures. The CC BY 4.0 license covers what this project adds: the compilation, the JSON format, the daily history and the texts.
